A useful shelf starts small

Start with five bottles.

You do not have to catalogue every drawer today. Start with the shades you reach for most, then add more as you wear or buy them.

1. Save the essentials

Type a brand and shade, choose a finish and color, and add a photo if it helps you recognize the bottle. Add your own brands without waiting for a catalog entry.

2. Check before shopping

Search your saved shelf or wishlist. The duplicate warning matches brand and shade names; it does not identify similar colors across different polishes.

3. Log your next wear

Tap Wearing today on a bottle you own. Your recent wear history and counts help you remember the shades you already enjoy.

Private by design

Your polish data isn't our business.

Lacquer Ledger has no account, backend, advertising, or analytics. Bottle details and photos stay on your device. Export creates a CSV of bottle details and wear fields; photos are not included.

Where is my collection stored?

In a local database inside the app on your device. Lacquer Ledger does not operate a server.

Can I make a portable copy?

Yes. Export bottle details and wear fields as a CSV file through your device's share sheet. Photos are not included, and version 1.0 does not import CSV files.

Is there a subscription or bottle limit?

It is one paid download, with no subscription or paid bottle-limit upgrade. The US App Store price is $4.99; local prices may vary.

Can I import a spreadsheet or scan barcodes?

Version 1.0 uses manual entry. It does not import spreadsheets, scan barcodes, or provide a shared polish catalog. Start with a few favorites; keep an existing spreadsheet if bulk entry is essential to you.

Does my collection sync or include a restorable backup?

Version 1.0 does not offer cloud sync or an in-app backup and restore feature. CSV export includes bottle details and wear fields, not photos. Save a CSV outside the app before deleting it or changing devices; the app cannot import that CSV.
