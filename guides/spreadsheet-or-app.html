Nail polish spreadsheet or collection app?

The useful collection list is the one you can check before shopping and update after a manicure. Choose around those two moments, and around the work of entering the bottles you already own.

Keep an established spreadsheet if bulk entry matters

If you already have hundreds of carefully entered records, you do not need to start again to keep a useful collection. Add the fields you actually consult: brand, shade, owned or wanted, finish, wear count and last worn date. Keep a copy in a place you can recover.

For a new inventory, the free inventory worksheet provides a fictional example and a blank record. It works without buying the app.

Try a small shelf before moving everything

  1. Record five bottles you own and one shade you want. Use the label's brand and shade names consistently.
  2. Before your next purchase, search that list. A matching name helps catch an exact duplicate; a similar color still needs your own comparison.
  3. After a manicure, record the wear. Next time, look for a bottle you have not used recently.
  4. After a week, decide whether you kept the list current. Expand it only if the routine helps you.

Understand the portable copy

Lacquer Ledger's CSV export includes brand, shade, finish, owned or wishlist status, color, wear count, most recent wear and notes. Photos are not included. Version 1.0 cannot import the file, so it is a portable text record, not a full restorable app backup. Save the exported file outside the app before deleting the app or changing devices.
